UNDERSTANDING ELIGIBILITY

What May Matter in a Business Finance Assessment?

Banks and finance providers apply their own credit policies. Eligibility is not determined by one factor alone. Depending on the product and lender, the following may be reviewed:

  • Business activity, legal structure and operating history
  • Annual turnover and transaction patterns
  • Bank account conduct and average balances
  • VAT returns and available financial statements
  • Existing liabilities and repayment obligations
  • Ownership details and relevant personal or business credit profile
  • Purpose of finance and proposed repayment capacity

Commonly Requested Documents

The exact checklist varies by lender and finance product. Common requests may include:

  • Valid trade licence and company documents
  • MOA or relevant ownership documents
  • Recent business bank statements
  • VAT returns, where applicable
  • Financial statements or management accounts
  • Shareholder and authorised signatory identification
  • Existing loan or liability details, if applicable

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Business Finance – FAQs

Does DUCES provide business loans directly?

No. DUCES is a consultancy service. We provide guidance, documentation assistance and coordination for businesses exploring finance from relevant financial institutions.

Can a newly established company apply for business finance?

Requirements differ by lender and product. Some finance products may require an established operating history, revenue records or other eligibility conditions. We can help you understand the information a lender may request.

Is a minimum turnover required?

There is no single minimum turnover applicable to every lender or finance product. Eligibility criteria vary and may also depend on profitability, account conduct, business activity and repayment capacity.

How long does a business finance application take?

Processing time depends on the lender, completeness of the documents, product and credit assessment. Timelines cannot be guaranteed.

Can DUCES guarantee loan approval?

No. Credit decisions, loan terms, limits and approvals are determined solely by the relevant financial institution. We do not guarantee approval or a specific financing amount.

Important: DUCES Consultancy Services is not a bank or financial institution and does not independently approve or disburse loans. Our role is limited to consultancy, documentation assistance and coordination. All finance applications are subject to the relevant institution's eligibility criteria, credit assessment, terms and final approval.
