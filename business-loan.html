Eligibility Preparation

Prepare Your Business Before Applying

Financial institutions independently assess each application. Their requirements may vary according to the business activity, company age, turnover, account conduct, financial statements and requested facility.

✓Valid UAE trade licence and company details
✓Business bank statements for the requested period
✓VAT returns and financial records, where applicable
✓Business profile, activity and ownership information
✓Clear explanation of funding purpose and expected use
✓Additional documents requested by the financial institution

What Can Affect an Assessment?

Business activity: The nature of the company's operations and its accepted business category.

Turnover and cash flow: Revenue consistency, transaction patterns and account conduct.

Company history: Business operating period and available financial records.

Existing obligations: Current loans, liabilities and repayment commitments.

Facility structure: Requested amount, tenure, security and other lender-specific conditions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Business Finance – Common Questions

Important points to understand before starting a finance enquiry.

Does DUCES directly provide business loans?

No. DUCES provides consultancy, documentation guidance and application coordination. Financing is provided and assessed by the relevant financial institution.

Is business finance approval guaranteed?

No. Approval is never guaranteed. Each institution independently reviews eligibility, documentation, credit assessment, business performance and its internal policies.

What documents are normally required?

Common documents may include a trade licence, company documents, partner identification, business bank statements, VAT records and financial statements. The exact list depends on the institution and facility.

Can a newly established company apply?

Requirements vary by institution and finance product. Some facilities require an established operating history, while other options may have different criteria. A review is needed before determining suitability.

How much finance can my company receive?

The amount, if any, depends on the lender's assessment of the business, turnover, cash flow, liabilities, security and other applicable criteria. DUCES does not promise a specific funding amount.

Does DUCES charge for consultancy?

Any applicable consultancy or service fee should be explained and agreed with the client before the service begins. Financial institution charges, if applicable, are separate.
