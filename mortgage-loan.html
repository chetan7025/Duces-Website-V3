APPLICANT PROFILE

What May Influence Mortgage Eligibility?

Every lender has its own assessment criteria. Depending on the applicant and property, a lender may consider:

  • Monthly income and employment or business profile
  • Existing loans, credit cards and other liabilities
  • Credit history and repayment conduct
  • Property value, type and location
  • Available down payment and source of funds
  • Applicant residency and relevant documentation
  • Loan amount, tenure and repayment capacity

Common Document Checklist

Requirements vary by lender and applicant category. Documents may include:

  • Passport, visa and Emirates ID, where applicable
  • Salary certificate or employment confirmation
  • Recent personal bank statements
  • Existing liability and credit information
  • Business and income documents for self-employed applicants
  • Property details, offer documents or sale agreement
  • Down payment and source-of-funds information

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Mortgage Finance – FAQs

Does DUCES provide mortgages directly?

No. DUCES is a consultancy service. We provide guidance, documentation support and coordination for customers exploring mortgage finance through relevant financial institutions.

Can non-residents explore UAE property finance?

Some lenders may consider non-resident applicants under specific products and conditions. Eligibility, available properties, down payment and documentation requirements vary by lender.

Can self-employed individuals apply for a mortgage?

Some lenders consider self-employed applicants, subject to business history, income evidence, financial records and their internal assessment criteria.

Can I explore refinancing or mortgage transfer?

You may explore these options with a lender. The decision depends on the outstanding finance, property valuation, applicant profile, fees and lender criteria.

Can DUCES guarantee mortgage approval or a specific interest rate?

No. Approval, rates, fees, loan amount and final terms are determined by the financial institution. We do not guarantee any financing outcome.

Important: DUCES Consultancy Services is not a bank, mortgage lender or financial institution. Our role is limited to consultancy, documentation assistance and coordination. Mortgage applications are subject to lender eligibility criteria, property valuation, credit assessment, applicable regulations and final approval. Any rates, fees or financing terms must be confirmed directly with the relevant financial institution.
