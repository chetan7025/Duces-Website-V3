Document Readiness

Documents Commonly Required for a Mortgage Enquiry

The exact documents depend on the applicant's employment or business profile, residency status, property type and financial institution. The following are common examples, not a fixed approval checklist.

✓Passport and valid UAE residency documents, where applicable
✓Emirates ID, where applicable
✓Salary certificate and recent payslips for salaried applicants
✓Personal bank statements for the requested period
✓Business licence and financial records for self-employed applicants
✓Property details, quotation, sale agreement or other relevant documents
✓Details of existing loans and financial commitments

Factors That May Influence Eligibility

Income profile: Salary, business income and consistency of earnings.

Existing commitments: Current loans, credit card obligations and other liabilities.

Applicant profile: Residency, employment or business history and lender-specific criteria.

Property details: Property value, location, type, status and valuation.

Down payment: The applicant's available contribution and applicable lender requirements.

Credit assessment: Credit history and the institution's internal risk assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Mortgage Finance – Common Questions

Some important points to consider before starting your property finance enquiry.

Does DUCES provide mortgage loans directly?

No. DUCES provides consultancy, document-readiness guidance and application coordination. Mortgage finance is provided and assessed by the relevant financial institution.

Can DUCES guarantee mortgage approval?

No. Approval is not guaranteed. The financial institution independently assesses the applicant, income, liabilities, property and its own lending criteria.

Can salaried and self-employed applicants enquire?

Both salaried and self-employed applicants may enquire. Their documentation and eligibility requirements can differ depending on the institution and finance product.

Can non-residents enquire about UAE property finance?

Some institutions may consider non-resident applicants under specific criteria. Availability, eligible properties, down payment and other conditions must be confirmed directly with the relevant institution.

How much down payment is required?

Down payment requirements vary according to applicant profile, property value, property type, residency status and applicable regulations and lender policies. No fixed percentage should be assumed for every applicant.

How long does the mortgage process take?

Timelines vary according to document readiness, valuation, property status, lender assessment and other parties involved. A specific completion date cannot be guaranteed.
