Before You Finalise Your Setup

Is the displayed amount my final business setup cost?

No. It is a starting package reference for selected DUCES packages. The final cost depends on the licensing authority, approved activities, visa allocation, office or desk requirement and any additional services.

Can I use this estimator for Mainland or Offshore setup?

Yes, to organise your requirements. The current DUCES package references shown here are not used for Mainland or Offshore routes, so those selections are marked for a tailored quotation.

Does selecting a visa guarantee visa approval?

No. Visa eligibility, allocation and approval are determined by the relevant authority and immigration requirements. DUCES can discuss coordination support but cannot guarantee approval.

What happens after I request a quotation?

DUCES reviews your selections, clarifies your activity and ownership requirements, and confirms the applicable package inclusions and additional charges before preparing a written quotation.
