ACCOUNT CONSIDERATIONS

What to review before approaching a bank

Requirements differ by bank, applicant profile, residency and account purpose. Final eligibility and approval remain with the bank.

01

Residency and eligibility

Banks may apply different criteria to UAE residents, non-residents and applicants with different visa or employment status.

02

Income and source of funds

Applicants may be asked to provide salary evidence, employment details, business income information or supporting source-of-funds documents.

03

Identity and address documents

Passport, Emirates ID, visa details and proof of address may be requested, depending on the bank and applicant profile.

04

Account purpose and activity

Expected transfers, cash usage, currencies and account purpose can influence which bank product is appropriate.
