DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Documents Commonly Required

The final checklist depends on the bank, company structure, business activity and individual application.

Company Documents

Trade Licence, Memorandum of Association (MOA), Certificate of Incorporation and other applicable company registration records.

Business Address

Ejari, tenancy agreement, office address evidence or other address documents as requested by the bank.

Shareholder KYC

Passport copies, Emirates ID, UAE visa information, contact details and relevant ownership information.

Business Information

Business activity details, expected turnover, transaction profile, source of funds and supporting financial or commercial records.

Document requirements vary. A bank may request additional information, clarification, business contracts, invoices, supplier details, customer information or other supporting records.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Business Bank Account in the UAE

Can a newly established company apply for a business account?

Some banks consider applications from newly established companies. Requirements depend on the bank, business activity, ownership and supporting profile.

Is a minimum balance required?

This depends on the selected account and bank. Some products may have balance conditions, monthly fees or other account charges.

Can a free zone company open a UAE business account?

Free zone companies may apply to banks that accept their company structure and business profile. Acceptance is subject to the bank's assessment.

How long does business account opening take?

Processing times vary according to the bank, application completeness, due diligence and any additional information requested.

Does DUCES guarantee account opening?

No. DUCES provides consultancy and coordination support. The bank independently conducts its checks and decides whether to open an account.

Can DUCES help compare banking options?

We can help discuss available options and commonly relevant requirements based on your business profile and stated needs.
