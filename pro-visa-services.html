FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

PRO & Visa Support – FAQs

Does DUCES issue UAE visas?

No. DUCES provides consultancy, documentation support and coordination. Visa decisions and issuance are handled by the relevant UAE government authorities.

Can you help with Emirates ID procedures?

We can explain the general process, help organise the relevant document checklist and coordinate applicable appointment or application steps with the appropriate channels.

Can you assist companies with employee visa requirements?

We can discuss the company's requirements and provide documentation and coordination support for applicable employee-related processes. Requirements depend on the company, jurisdiction and applicant category.

How early should I plan a visa renewal?

It is sensible to review expiry dates in advance. The appropriate timeline depends on the visa category and current authority procedures. Contact us with the relevant details so we can help identify the next steps.

Can you guarantee visa approval?

No. Approval, processing time and final decisions are determined by the relevant government authorities. We do not guarantee approvals or processing outcomes.

Important: DUCES Consultancy Services provides consultancy, documentation assistance and coordination support. We are not a government authority and do not represent that applications will be approved. All applications remain subject to the applicable laws, eligibility criteria and decisions of the relevant UAE authorities.
