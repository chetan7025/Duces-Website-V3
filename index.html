COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful information before you discuss your requirements with us.

Does DUCES help with business bank account applications?

Yes. DUCES provides consultancy, documentation guidance and application coordination support. The bank independently assesses every application and makes the final decision.

Can DUCES help me choose between Free Zone and Mainland?

We can discuss your proposed activity, ownership, operational needs and visa requirements to help you understand the relevant setup routes. Final licensing requirements are determined by the applicable authority.

Does DUCES guarantee business loans or bank account approval?

No. Banks and financial institutions independently assess applications, eligibility and supporting documents. DUCES provides consultancy and coordination support, not approval guarantees.

Can an existing UAE company approach DUCES for support?

Yes. Existing businesses can enquire about banking, finance, accounting, bookkeeping, VAT, Corporate Tax and relevant documentation coordination services.

How do I get started?

Submit the enquiry form or contact DUCES through WhatsApp. Share your requirement and we can discuss the relevant next steps.
