UAE COMPANY SETUP

Mainland, Free Zone or Offshore?

The appropriate structure depends on what you do, where you operate and what your business needs.

Consideration Mainland Free Zone Offshore
Typical use Operating in the UAE market, subject to activity rules Activities within the selected free zone framework Specific holding or international structuring purposes
Office needs Depends on activity and authority Package and authority dependent Usually different from operating licence requirements
Visa options Subject to licence and immigration rules Package and space dependent Generally not designed for UAE residence visas
Banking Subject to bank assessment Subject to bank assessment May involve additional bank due diligence

This is a high-level guide, not a legal or licensing determination. Rules, costs and eligibility vary by authority, activity and individual circumstances.
