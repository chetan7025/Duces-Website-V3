FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Business Setup in the UAE

What is the difference between free zone and mainland?

They are different licensing and operating structures. The suitable route depends on your business activity, customer base and operational requirements.

Can a foreign investor own a UAE company?

Foreign ownership is permitted for many business activities, subject to the applicable legal framework, activity and licensing authority requirements.

Does every company setup include a residence visa?

No. Visa eligibility and allocation depend on the licence package, jurisdiction, immigration rules and applicable requirements.

Can DUCES help with business bank account enquiries?

DUCES can provide consultancy and documentation coordination support. Account opening remains subject to the bank's independent assessment.

What documents may be required?

Requirements vary. Common examples include passport copies, contact details, proposed activity information and relevant shareholder documents.

How much does company formation cost?

Costs vary by authority, activity, licence type, visa allocation and other selected services. A quotation should be based on your requirements.
