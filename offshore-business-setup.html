IMPORTANT CONSIDERATIONS

What Should Be Reviewed Before You Proceed?

Jurisdiction & Registered Agent

Compare available jurisdictions, registered agent requirements, annual renewals and the authority's permitted company activities.

Ownership & Documentation

Review shareholder and director details, beneficial ownership information, identity documents and source-of-funds evidence.

Banking & Compliance

Offshore incorporation does not guarantee a bank account. Banks conduct their own due diligence and may request evidence of business purpose and economic substance.

Tax & Reporting

Consider tax residency, reporting obligations and any applicable substance or disclosure requirements with qualified professional advice.

UAE Operating Activity

An offshore company is generally not designed as a substitute for a UAE mainland or free zone operating licence. Confirm the permitted scope before trading in the UAE.

Visa & Office Needs

Offshore structures are generally not intended to provide UAE residence visas or ordinary office facilities. Confirm the rules with the relevant authority.

Important: Offshore company rules, eligibility, fees, renewal requirements, banking access and tax treatment vary by jurisdiction and applicant circumstances.
