WHO WE ARE

Helping You Navigate Business Requirements in the UAE

Starting, managing and growing a business involves more than one decision. Entrepreneurs may need to consider company formation, banking, finance, accounting, taxation and ongoing administrative requirements.

DUCES brings these service areas together through a consultancy-led approach. Our objective is to help clients understand their requirements, organise relevant information and coordinate the appropriate next steps.

We focus on clear communication, practical guidance and a structured process so clients can make informed decisions about their business requirements.

OUR ROLE

One Consultancy Point of Contact

We help connect the different parts of a business journey: understanding the requirement, identifying relevant options, preparing documentation and coordinating with the appropriate service providers or institutions.

OUR PRINCIPLES

OUR COMMITMENT

Clear Scope. Realistic Expectations.

A discussion focused on their requirements, clear information about the proposed scope of support, guidance on relevant documentation and coordination of agreed next steps.
