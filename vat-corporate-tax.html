UAE VAT – General Reference

Standard VAT rate: The standard UAE VAT rate is 5%, subject to the applicable rules and treatment of supplies.

Mandatory registration: The general mandatory registration threshold is AED 375,000 of taxable supplies and imports over the relevant period.

Voluntary registration: The general voluntary registration threshold is AED 187,500, subject to the applicable conditions.

Important: Threshold calculations and registration eligibility depend on the specific facts and current FTA rules. Confirm your position before taking action.

Corporate Tax – General Reference

0% rate: Taxable income up to AED 375,000 is generally subject to a 0% Corporate Tax rate.

9% rate: Taxable income above AED 375,000 is generally subject to a 9% rate.

Free Zone entities: Qualifying Free Zone Persons may benefit from a 0% rate on qualifying income, subject to the applicable conditions.

Important: Tax treatment depends on the entity, income type, eligibility and current legislation. A 0% rate does not automatically mean exemption from registration or filing obligations.

Frequently Asked Questions

VAT & Corporate Tax – Common Questions

General answers to common UAE business tax questions.

What is the standard VAT rate in the UAE?

The standard VAT rate is generally 5%. Certain supplies may be zero-rated, exempt or subject to specific rules.

When does a business need to register for VAT?

The general mandatory registration threshold is AED 375,000 of taxable supplies and imports over the relevant period. Voluntary registration may be available from AED 187,500, subject to applicable conditions.

Does every UAE company need to register for Corporate Tax?

Corporate Tax registration requirements depend on the type of entity and applicable rules. Some entities may have different treatment or exemptions. Businesses should verify their position with the Federal Tax Authority or an appropriately qualified adviser.

Is Corporate Tax always 9%?

No. The general rate is 0% on taxable income up to AED 375,000 and 9% on taxable income above that amount. Special rules and qualifying Free Zone provisions may apply.

Does a Free Zone company automatically qualify for 0% Corporate Tax?

No. A Free Zone company must satisfy the conditions for Qualifying Free Zone Person status and qualifying income treatment. The applicable rules and conditions must be reviewed individually.

Can DUCES help organise VAT and Corporate Tax records?

DUCES can discuss record organisation, documentation guidance and coordination support within the agreed service scope. Specialist tax advice or regulated services will be referred to an appropriately qualified professional where required.

Are tax registration and filing deadlines the same for every company?

No. Deadlines and obligations depend on the entity, tax period and applicable rules. Businesses should verify their specific deadlines and maintain a compliance calendar.
