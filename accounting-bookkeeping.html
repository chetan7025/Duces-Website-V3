Frequently Asked Questions

Accounting & Bookkeeping – Common Questions

Answers to common questions about maintaining business records in the UAE.

What is the difference between accounting and bookkeeping?

Bookkeeping focuses on recording and organising financial transactions. Accounting involves reviewing, classifying and interpreting financial information and preparing relevant reports.

Can a newly established company use bookkeeping services?

Yes. A new company can establish a record-keeping process from the beginning, including organising invoices, receipts, bank transactions and expenses.

How often should bookkeeping be updated?

The appropriate frequency depends on transaction volume, business activity and reporting needs. Regular updates generally make financial records easier to review.

Do you support VAT and Corporate Tax requirements?

DUCES can help organise accounting records and coordinate relevant VAT and Corporate Tax consultancy enquiries. The specific service and any required regulated or specialist work should be confirmed before engagement.

Do you provide statutory audits?

This page describes bookkeeping and accounting support, not statutory audit services. Where an audit is required, the company should engage an appropriately authorised audit firm.

What documents should a business maintain?

Businesses should maintain relevant invoices, receipts, bank statements, contracts, payment records and other supporting documents according to their activities and applicable record-retention requirements.
