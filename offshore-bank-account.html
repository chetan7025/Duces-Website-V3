Offshore Bank Account Guidance

Offshore banking is not a one-size-fits-all solution. Jurisdiction, tax residency, beneficial ownership, source of wealth, business purpose and the bank's risk policy all matter. DUCES helps clients understand these considerations before approaching a financial institution.

ACCOUNT CONSIDERATIONS

What to review before approaching a bank

Requirements differ by bank, applicant profile, residency and account purpose. Final eligibility and approval remain with the bank.

01

Purpose and jurisdiction

Clarify why an offshore account is being considered and whether the proposed jurisdiction and account type fit the legitimate purpose.

02

Ownership and beneficial owners

Banks commonly require transparent information about account holders, directors, shareholders and ultimate beneficial owners.

03

Source of wealth and funds

Prepare clear evidence explaining how funds were accumulated and the expected origin and destination of transactions.

04

Tax residency and compliance

Consider tax residency, reporting obligations, exchange of information rules and local legal advice before proceeding.
